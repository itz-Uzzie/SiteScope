Grow a healthy home garden

Gardening can be simple when you begin with a plan. Choose a sunny space, learn about your soil, and select plants that suit your local weather. A little care every week helps your garden stay healthy and rewarding through the seasons.

Choose the right location

Most vegetables need several hours of direct sunlight each day. Watch your yard during the morning and afternoon before choosing a spot for your beds.

Prepare the soil

Healthy soil supports healthy plants. Add compost, remove weeds, and make sure water can drain away after a storm.
